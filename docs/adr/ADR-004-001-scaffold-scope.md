# ADR-004-001 Scaffold Scope

Decision: MILESTONE-004 initializes only foundation scaffolding and generic platform interfaces.

Rationale: MILESTONE-003 authorizes repository scaffolding but prohibits business logic, empirical validation, vendor adapters, B3 criteria, statistical logic, UI, and production APIs beyond health/version placeholders.

Reversal condition: a future milestone explicitly authorizes a business or infrastructure slice.
